Detailed Book Information

The Alchemist

Author: Paulo Coelho

Publisher: HarperCollins

Genre: Fiction / Adventure

Full Summary

The Alchemist follows Santiago, a young shepherd who dreams of finding a hidden treasure. His journey teaches him about dreams, determination, and discovering one's purpose in life.

Why Read This Book?

  • Inspirational life lessons
  • Simple and meaningful storytelling
  • Encourages personal growth
