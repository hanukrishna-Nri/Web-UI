  📜 Right to Information (RTI)

   Every citizen has the right to seek information from any government office under the RTI Act, 2005.

Step Action Details
1 Write RTI Application Address to the Public Information Officer (PIO) of the concerned department
2 Pay Application Fee ₹10 by postal order / DD / online payment
3 Submit Application By post, in person, or through rtionline.gov.in
4 Receive Response Department must reply within 30 days (48 hours for life/liberty matters)
5 File First Appeal (if needed) Appeal to First Appellate Authority within 30 days of reply
6 File Second Appeal (if needed) Appeal to AP State Information Commission at apic.ap.gov.in
